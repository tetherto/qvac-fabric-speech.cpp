Reading the numbers

Speed × real time = audio duration / processing time; higher is better. RTF = processing time / audio duration; lower is better. Competitor ÷ QVAC time is the competitor's time divided by QVAC's time on the same setup. Above 1 means QVAC took less time.

ASR, Supertonic and Nemotron rows time inference only. Audio8, MOSS-TTS and ACE-Step rows are whole fresh processes including model load, so their speed is a throughput figure, not a latency. Variants generate audio of slightly different length for the same prompt, so each comparison shows latency, generated duration and speed together.

About these measurements and interpretation notes

TTS WER is intelligibility: pinned Whisper tiny transcribes the generated clip outside the timed region. It includes scorer errors and does not measure naturalness or speaker similarity. Fresh-process timings include model loading from a warm filesystem cache; they are not cold-disk startup times. MOSS-TTS Q8_0 is the F16 backbone with its transformer blocks requantized on the machine, because the registry has no Q8_0 cut.

—

Music generation

A 30 s instrumental render at seed 42 as a fresh process including the load of all four stages: ACE-Step 1.5 Turbo with the DiT at Q8_0 and at Q4_K_M, SFT at Q8_0 and Base at Q4_K_M, each with the Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B Q8_0, the 5Hz LM 0.6B Q8_0 and the BF16 VAE. The campaign charts compare QVAC with acestep.cpp on ACE-Step Turbo over the campaign's ten prompts.
